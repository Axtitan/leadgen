      TomTom key (free)

      Finds far more businesses than OpenStreetMap, with phones and websites, and needs no card. About 2,500 searches a month free; when they run out, searches stop until next month and nothing is charged.

      1. Create a free account at developer.tomtom.com.
      2. In your Dashboard, open the key and make sure Search API is ticked.
      3. Paste the key below.

      Find missing websites

      OpenStreetMap and TomTom often leave a business's website out. For every lead with no website listed, LeadFinder tries the obvious web addresses for their name, and only keeps one that shows their phone number, or their name and town.

      For the most reliable check, add a free Apify token. LeadFinder then looks the rest up on Google Maps and takes the website, rating and review count from their Google profile. Apify gives $5 of free credit every month with no card, which covers roughly 1,000 businesses; the strongest leads are checked first. When the credit runs out, checks pause until next month and nothing is charged.

      1. Sign up at apify.com (no card needed).
      2. In the Apify Console, open Settings → API & Integrations and copy your personal API token.
      3. Paste it below. Your No website leads are checked straight away, about 40 at a time.

      Google key (optional, paid past the free amount)

      Google adds ratings and review counts, which helps pick the busiest businesses. It needs a billing account. Enable Places API (New), plus PageSpeed Insights API for speed tests.
